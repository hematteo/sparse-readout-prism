\section{Robustness and Fidelity Controls}
\label{app:robustness-controls}

This appendix collects the controls needed to interpret and reproduce the local
score accounts: tokenization and filtering rules, row-norm and frequency checks,
split construction, dictionary settings, and aggregate metrics. The accounting
definitions themselves are stated in Appendix~\ref{app:score-accounting}.

\subsection{Key Quantitative Results}
\label{app:robustness-key-quantitative}

The key quantitative result is that the readout SAE evaluation separates broad
readout replacement from target-level fidelity. On the all-row target set,
aggregating both held-out halves, the Qwen3.5-0.8B/2B/9B settings give sign
agreement \(0.940\)--\(0.951\), median \(\rho=0.091\)--\(0.107\), and
\(\rho<0.5\) fractions \(0.790\)--\(0.818\). The main-text fidelity table
reports the prespecified high-fidelity subset of this evaluation, which is why
its Qwen sign and reconstruction error ranges are slightly tighter; it also
reports Gemma target-fidelity rows and Ministral/R1 replacement rows under the
same metric conventions. The baseline/null table fixes Qwen3.5-2B and evaluates
every method on its 1,348-row full logit-difference set. The C4 replacement
evaluation is harder because many positions are local near-ties, but Qwen still
gives \(0.822\)--\(0.838\) top-1 agreement and \(0.634\)--\(0.667\)
preservation of the top-1--top-2 ordering at relative error \(0.5\) on 10,000
neutral C4 continuations per model
\citep{raffel2020exploring,dodge2021documenting}. For confident Qwen margins,
\(|m_{\mathrm{exact}}|\ge2\), C4 top-1 agreement rises to \(0.97\)--\(1.00\)
and top-1--top-2 ordering preservation to \(0.84\)--\(0.96\). Additional
model-family rows use the same metric suite and separate row reconstruction,
replacement fidelity, and target-level reconstruction. Null and reference
baseline comparisons preserve the same ordering: shuffled and random support
approach chance sign agreement, and dense PCA underperforms Sparse Readout Prism
at comparable compactness. The baseline panel in
Figure~\ref{fig:app-robustness-baseline-comparisons} reports the same target set
and metrics as the Sparse Readout Prism row. Across the target-group and
baseline comparisons, tokenization filters, row-norm controls, frequency
controls, KL, sign agreement, and relative reconstruction error are evaluated
with the same definitions.

\subsection{Operating-Regime Cases}
\label{app:robustness-operating-regime}

Operating-regime cases define when a compact local score account is
evidence-bearing. Qualitative interpretation uses cases where the target score is
larger than the reconstruction error, the reconstructed contrast preserves sign,
the exact model readout supports the intended target, contribution mass is
compact enough to summarize, and token-boundary, special-token, row-norm,
frequency, or tokenization-collision effects do not dominate the contrast. The
remaining cases are retained as quantitative records under the same reporting
rule.
