\begin{abstract}
Lens-style analyses map hidden states through the final unembedding into
vocabulary logits, but a selected logit or contrast is one dense score. It does
not say which directions in the readout raise or lower that score, nor how
much error a sparse account would incur. We introduce Sparse Readout Prism
(SRP), which factorizes LM-head rows into sparse token-row coefficients over
shared directions. Given a hidden state and a chosen score, such as a token
logit or logit difference, SRP decomposes that score into signed feature
contributions plus an explicit residual in original logit coordinates.

In high-fidelity Qwen settings, SRP preserves held-out contrast signs at
\(0.949\)--\(0.958\) and keeps median relative reconstruction error below
\(0.10\); on Qwen3.5-2B it cuts median error from \(0.260\) (best baseline) to
\(0.081\). The same fixed basis then lets us compare score choices, prompt
contexts, residual-stream components, and constrained readout-side lexical-score
edits within one decomposition.
\end{abstract}
