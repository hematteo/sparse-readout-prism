\begin{abstract}
Lens-style analyses map hidden states through the final unembedding into
vocabulary logits, but a selected logit or contrast is one dense score. It does
not say which directions in the readout support or oppose the target, nor how
much error a sparse account would incur. We introduce Sparse Readout Prism
(SRP), which factorizes LM-head rows into sparse token-row coefficients over
shared directions. Given a hidden state and a scalar target, SRP decomposes the
score into signed feature contributions plus an explicit residual in original
logit coordinates.

Across model families and readout geometries, SRP preserves held-out contrast
signs at \(0.949\)--\(0.958\), keeps median relative reconstruction error below
\(0.10\) in high-fidelity settings, and cuts median error from \(0.260\) (best
baseline) to \(0.081\). The same fixed basis then lets us compare target
choices, prompt contexts, residual-stream components, and constrained
readout-side edits within one decomposition.
\end{abstract}
