\section{Conclusion}

Sparse Readout Prism offers an alternative unit for logit-lens-style
interpretation of vocabulary readouts. Token identity can be too coarse
where one token draws on several structures and too fine where several
tokens share one; SRP complements the token-only account with a
shared, compositional one in readout features, separating what any selected readout score
asks about from what the hidden state supplies, with an explicit
residual in original logit units. The representation
reconstructs selected scores where direct row geometry does not,
reproduces across independent runs, predicts the measured effects of
local readout-side edits, and exposes what token rankings cannot---contexts
recruiting different features for one token, and differently fitted
lenses reporting different surface languages while one readout feature
stays dominant (corpus conditionality). Accounts remain local to a selected score; a surface-token
reading, from any lens, is only as trustworthy as the feature account
beneath it.
