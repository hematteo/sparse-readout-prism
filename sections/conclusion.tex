\section{Conclusion}

Sparse Readout Prism separates what is fixed by a sparse readout basis from
what is chosen by the score and what is supplied by context. Replacement
fidelity, score reconstruction, sign agreement, and baseline comparisons decide
when the factorized readout is accurate enough to interpret. Under those gates,
SRP supports readout-level comparisons across score choices, contexts,
residual-stream components, and candidate lexical edits; outside them, feature
labels alone are not evidence.

More broadly, researchers can use SRP to decompose a selected lens score into
coordinate terms that support or oppose it, together with the residual left by
the approximation. The same template extends along several axes. Other linear
readouts --- reward-model heads, classifier heads, and retrieval projections
--- admit the same factorization and gates, carrying readout-level analyses
into alignment and retrieval settings. Tuned-lens and logit-prism readouts
taken across depth give per-layer feature bases, exposing where in the
residual stream a feature first becomes readout-accessible and how that depth
signature varies across score families. Activation-level interventions on
individual decoder directions can test whether feature support implies causal
responsibility, complementing the additive accounting in feature-resolved DLA
with sufficiency and necessity checks. Cross-checkpoint comparisons of the
learned bases ask which features are stable across training and which appear,
drift, or collapse over the course of pretraining and fine-tuning. A
residual-stream census measures how projection mass divides between the
selected score, competing scores, and directions that are active but locally
unused; comparing this split across layers, prompts, and correct and
incorrect generations tests whether models carry broad task state beyond the
immediate readout decision. Throughout, replacement fidelity, score
reconstruction, and sign agreement remain the gate: feature labels become
evidence only where the factorized readout reproduces what it is meant to
interpret.
