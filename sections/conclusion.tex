\section{Conclusion}

Sparse Readout Prism separates the score-independent and score-conditioned parts
of readout decomposition. It factorizes LM-head rows into shared readout
features and token-row coefficients; after a token logit, logit difference, or
other fixed row-combination score fixes the readout direction and sign
convention, hidden states enter through feature projections that yield signed
terms plus residuals in raw logit coordinates. Fidelity diagnostics identify
settings where these terms preserve readout scores. In those settings, the fixed
SRP basis supports score/context comparisons, feature-resolved attribution, and
candidate directions for constrained readout-side lexical-score edits.

\paragraph{Future work.}
SRP applies to any effective readout. Extending it to tuned lenses,
logit-prism variants, and future-token lenses would enable layer-by-layer
comparison of decoded tokens, signed feature terms, and residuals.
