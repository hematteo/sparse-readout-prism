\section{Conclusion}

We introduced Sparse Readout Prism as a readout-side sparse factorization and
tested when its induced local accounts remain faithful to the original LM head.
The central object is an accounting basis for selected logits, contrasts, and
margins: feature terms are reported together with offset and residual terms in
the model's raw logit coordinates.

The experiments first establish the operating regime. LM-head replacement
fidelity, target reconstruction, sign agreement, and support baselines identify
settings where the sparse factorization preserves readout behavior and selected
scalar targets. The qualitative sections then use those settings to compare
target choices, prompt contexts, feature-resolved residual-stream attribution,
and constrained readout-side edits.

SRP therefore moves lens-style readouts from atomic vocabulary scores to
feature-resolved readout decompositions. The same signed terms apply beyond
curated token pairs, compose with direct logit attribution, and can select
readout-side directions that move held-out lexical scores. SRP does not by
itself establish causal mechanisms or sequence-level behavior, but it supplies a
calibrated readout-linear basis for asking those questions with explicit
residuals and fidelity diagnostics.
