\section{Conclusion}

Sparse Readout Prism separates the target-independent and target-conditioned
parts of readout decomposition. It factorizes LM-head rows into shared readout
features and token-row coefficients; after a scalar readout target fixes the
linear score and sign convention, hidden states enter through feature
projections that yield signed terms plus residuals in raw logit coordinates.
Fidelity diagnostics identify settings where these terms preserve readout
behavior. In those settings, the fixed SRP basis supports target/context
comparisons, feature-resolved attribution, and candidate directions for
constrained readout-side edits.

\paragraph{Future work.}
SRP applies to any effective readout. Extending it to tuned lenses,
logit-prism variants, and future-token lenses would enable layer-by-layer
comparison of decoded tokens, signed feature terms, and residuals.
