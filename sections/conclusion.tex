\section{Conclusion}

Sparse Readout Prism factorizes the LM head to turn selected readout scores into
signed feature-level accounts with explicit residuals in raw logit coordinates.
Replacement, target-reconstruction, sign, and baseline diagnostics identify
settings where those accounts preserve readout behavior. In those settings, SRP
compares targets and contexts, composes with residual-stream attribution, and
supplies directions for constrained readout-side edits. SRP does not replace
causal intervention or generation-level evaluation, but it provides an auditable
readout-linear basis for choosing where those evaluations should focus.
