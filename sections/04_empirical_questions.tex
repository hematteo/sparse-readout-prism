\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

The method defines two linked objects: a sparse factorization of the final LM
head and a scalar-target interface that turns readout feature projections into
signed local terms. The results follow that dependency chain. We first test
whether the factorized readout preserves LM-head behavior on held-out hidden
states, then ask whether learned token-to-feature support reconstructs selected
targets beyond what sparse capacity alone provides. With this calibrated
operating point, we show that the scalar-target formalism covers readout
targets beyond curated token pairs. Finally, we use the resulting terms to
separate target choice from context reweighting and to compose SRP with
residual-stream attribution and constrained readout-side edits. For signed
feature accounts, we report whether the sparse reconstruction preserves the
original sign and its local relative error \(\rho\); unless otherwise stated,
aggregate low-error counts use \(\rho<0.5\).
