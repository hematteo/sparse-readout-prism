\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

The results test four claims. First, SRP reconstructs selected readout targets
with high sign fidelity in identifiable regimes. Second, learned
token-to-feature support contributes beyond sparse capacity. Third, the
same hidden-state profile yields different accounts under different scalar
targets and prompt contexts. Fourth, the resulting feature terms compose with
residual-stream attribution and narrow readout-side edits.
For signed feature accounts, we report whether the sparse reconstruction
preserves the original sign and its local relative error \(\rho\); unless
otherwise stated, aggregate low-error counts use \(\rho<0.5\).
