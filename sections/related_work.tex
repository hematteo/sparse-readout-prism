\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Lens-style readout interfaces.}
The logit lens maps hidden states through the model's unembedding
\citep{nostalgebraist2020}; the tuned lens learns layerwise translators to
calibrate those predictions \citep{belrose2023tuned}. Future Lens asks whether
a single hidden state predicts later tokens \citep{pal2023future}, and
Patchscopes elicits continuations or descriptions by patching representations
into prompted contexts \citep{ghandeharioun2024patchscopes}. These methods
vary the hidden-state inspection interface; SRP instead leaves the selected
readout direction in place and decomposes it into sparse readout features.

\paragraph{Output geometry and sparse row dictionaries.}
Output-embedding work studies how weight tying
\citep{press2017outputembedding,inan2017tying}, softmax rank
\citep{yang2018softmaxbottleneck}, and tokenizer segmentation
\citep{bostrom2020bpe} shape the rows being scored. Activation-SAE work
establishes sparse dictionaries over hidden-state distributions
\citep{bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2023sparse,gao2024scaling}, with
standardized diagnostics \citep{karvonen2025saebench,makelov2024principled}.
SRP applies this sparse-dictionary vocabulary to LM-head rows rather than
hidden states. Concurrent anonymous work studies unembedding dynamics across
training checkpoints via parameter-trajectory crosscoding
\citep{anonymous2026learningreadout}; SRP instead studies a fixed LM-head
factorization in trained models.

\paragraph{Additive attribution and mechanisms.}
Direct logit attribution decomposes residual-stream components against token
logits or logit-difference directions
\citep{elhage2021framework,wang2022ioi}, and logit-prism methods extend this
across transformer components \citep{nguyen2024logitprisms}. SRP further
decomposes the selected readout direction into sparse readout features,
yielding feature-resolved DLA on a realized forward pass. This remains
additive accounting; intervention methods \citep{conmy2023acdc}, causal
abstraction \citep{geiger2023causalabstraction}, and transcoder or
attribution-graph approaches
\citep{dunefsky2024transcoders,ameisen2025circuittracing} address
circuit- and mechanism-level questions that SRP does not. Broader positioning
appears in Appendix~\ref{app:extended-related-work}.
