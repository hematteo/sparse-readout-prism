\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Lens-style readouts.}
Lens-style methods inspect hidden states by mapping them to vocabulary
distributions, future-token predictions, continuations, or descriptions
\citep{nostalgebraist2020,belrose2023tuned,pal2023future,ghandeharioun2024patchscopes}.
They are the closest prior work in interface: they ask what a hidden state reads
out. SRP keeps this interface but factorizes the final readout; selected scalar
targets then decompose a fixed logit or contrast into sparse readout-feature
terms plus residual. This does not replace learned or task-specific lenses; it
gives residual-checked decompositions for scores from the model's final readout.

\paragraph{Output-embedding geometry.}
SRP factorizes the final unembedding, or LM head. Output embeddings have been
studied as learned representations and prediction-shaping geometry
\citep{press2017outputembedding,inan2017tying,yang2018softmaxbottleneck,bostrom2020bpe}.
Token rows, weight tying, softmax rank constraints, and vocabulary segmentation
affect final scores. SRP uses this geometry as the object of a sparse row
factorization; selected targets combine row coefficients to decompose linear
readout scores at hidden states, without removing tokenizer or output-geometry
caveats.

\paragraph{Sparse dictionaries and activation SAEs.}
Activation-SAE work learns dictionaries over hidden-state distributions and has
established evaluation practice for sparse features
\citep{bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2023sparse,gao2024scaling,karvonen2025saebench,makelov2024principled}.
SRP borrows SAE machinery, sparse codes, decoder directions, and reconstruction
diagnostics, but changes the fitted object. The readout SAE is trained on
unembedding rows: its sparse codes are token-row coefficients, its decoder
directions are readout feature directions, and \(h^\top d_i\) is a readout
feature projection rather than an activation-SAE code. Features become signed
contributions only after a scalar readout target supplies coefficients, so the
row factorization alone does not recover activation features or hidden-state
mechanisms.

\paragraph{Logit attribution and logit prisms.}
Component attribution and logit-prism methods ask where logit evidence is
written in the residual stream
\citep{elhage2021framework,wang2022ioi,nguyen2024logitprisms}. The
selected-target layer asks a complementary readout-side question: which sparse
directions in the selected readout direction support or oppose the score.
Because target-conditioned terms are linear in the hidden state, they compose
with residual-stream attribution to yield feature-resolved DLA for a realized
forward pass. This is additive accounting for readout scores, not
identification of the upstream mechanism that produced a component
contribution.

\paragraph{Circuits and causal structure.}
Automated circuit discovery, causal abstraction, transcoders, and
circuit-tracing systems pursue internal mechanisms and intervention-tested
structure
\citep{conmy2023acdc,geiger2023causalabstraction,dunefsky2024transcoders,ameisen2025circuittracing}.
SRP differs in level of analysis: it factorizes the rows against which hidden
states are scored and reports residual-checked local score terms. The resulting
directions can suggest candidate interventions, but necessity and sufficiency
remain causal questions for intervention-tested methods.
