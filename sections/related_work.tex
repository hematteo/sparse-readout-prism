\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Lens-style readout interfaces.}
The logit lens maps hidden states through the model's unembedding
\citep{nostalgebraist2020}; the tuned lens learns layerwise translators to
calibrate those predictions \citep{belrose2023tuned}. Future Lens asks whether
a single hidden state predicts later tokens \citep{pal2023future}, and
Patchscopes elicits continuations or descriptions by patching representations
into prompted contexts \citep{ghandeharioun2024patchscopes}. These methods
vary how the hidden state is read out; SRP keeps the readout interface and
decomposes the selected LM-head direction into sparse readout features.

\paragraph{Output geometry and sparse row dictionaries.}
Output-embedding work studies how weight tying
\citep{press2017outputembedding,inan2017tying}, softmax rank
\citep{yang2018softmaxbottleneck}, and tokenizer segmentation
\citep{bostrom2020bpe} shape the rows used for vocabulary scoring. A
parallel sparse-dictionary line targets the hidden-state factor \(h\) of a
lens score \(h^\top q\): activation-SAEs learn overcomplete bases for \(h\)
\citep{bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2023sparse,gao2024scaling}, with
standardized diagnostics
\citep{karvonen2025saebench,makelov2024principled}. SRP applies the same
machinery to the complementary factor \(q\), fitting it directly to LM-head
rows. It therefore produces an inference-time basis for local score
decompositions, feature-resolved DLA, and readout-side edits.

\paragraph{Additive attribution and mechanisms.}
Direct logit attribution splits a token logit or logit-difference direction
into contributions from residual-stream components
\citep{elhage2021framework,wang2022ioi}, and logit-prism methods extend this
across transformer components \citep{nguyen2024logitprisms}. Composing the
row-side basis with DLA yields feature-resolved attributions on a realized
forward pass, refining each component's scalar contribution into signed
per-feature terms that lens-style methods leave as a single dense score.
The combined view remains additive; interventions \citep{conmy2023acdc}, causal
abstraction \citep{geiger2023causalabstraction}, and transcoder or
attribution-graph approaches
\citep{dunefsky2024transcoders,ameisen2025circuittracing} address the
complementary circuit- and mechanism-level questions. Broader positioning
appears in Appendix~\ref{app:extended-related-work}.
