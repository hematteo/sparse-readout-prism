\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Lens-style readout interfaces.}
SRP keeps the lens-style readout interface but factorizes the model's own LM
head behind it: a selected token logit or contrast remains a dot product with
an unembedding row or row combination, and SRP decomposes that readout
direction into sparse feature terms with residual checks. Prior lens methods
map hidden states to vocabulary distributions, future-token predictions,
continuations, or descriptions
\citep{nostalgebraist2020,belrose2023tuned,pal2023future,ghandeharioun2024patchscopes},
leaving the readout direction itself unfactorized.

\paragraph{Output geometry and sparse row dictionaries.}
SRP applies sparse-dictionary machinery to a different object from prior
activation-SAE work: unembedding rows. Its sparse codes are token-row
coefficients, its decoder directions are readout feature directions, and
\(h^\top d_i\) is a hidden-state projection onto that readout basis, not an
activation-SAE code. Output embeddings have been studied as learned
representations and prediction-shaping geometry
\citep{press2017outputembedding,inan2017tying,yang2018softmaxbottleneck,bostrom2020bpe}:
token rows, weight tying, softmax rank constraints, and vocabulary
segmentation all affect final scores; activation-SAE work has established
sparse-feature dictionaries and diagnostics over hidden-state distributions
\citep{bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2023sparse,gao2024scaling,karvonen2025saebench,makelov2024principled}.

\paragraph{Additive attribution and mechanisms.}
SRP asks the complementary readout-side question to component attribution:
which sparse readout directions raise or lower a selected score. The
selected-direction terms are linear in the hidden state and so compose with
residual-stream attribution to yield feature-resolved DLA for a realized
forward pass. Component attribution and logit-prism methods locate where logit
evidence is written in the residual stream
\citep{elhage2021framework,wang2022ioi,nguyen2024logitprisms} using standard
readout objects such as token logits and logit-difference directions; this
is additive accounting for readout scores, not intervention-tested mechanism
discovery
\citep{conmy2023acdc,geiger2023causalabstraction,dunefsky2024transcoders,ameisen2025circuittracing}.
Broader positioning appears in Appendix~\ref{app:extended-related-work}.
