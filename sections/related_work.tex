\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Lens-style readout interfaces.}
The logit lens decodes hidden states through the model's unembedding
\citep{nostalgebraist2020}. Tuned, Future-Lens, Patchscopes, and
Jacobian-lens variants learn or transport the map before decoding
\citep{belrose2023tuned,pal2023future,ghandeharioun2024patchscopes,gurnee2026workspace}.
All report tokens, and the fitted variants inherit a fitting corpus. SRP
keeps the readout interface but decomposes the selected LM-head
direction into sparse readout features, so differently fitted lenses can
be compared on the same state (\Cref{subsec:cross-lens}).

\paragraph{Output geometry and sparse row dictionaries.}
Weight tying
\citep{press2017outputembedding,inan2017tying,lopardo2026weighttying},
softmax and target geometry
\citep{yang2018softmaxbottleneck,zhao2024implicit}, and tokenizer
segmentation \citep{bostrom2020bpe} shape the vocabulary rows, and
sparse coding of static word embeddings recovers interpretable codes
from type-level rows
\citep{murphy2012nnse,faruqui2015sparse,subramanian2018spine,arora2018polysemy}.
SRP carries this row-side approach to the LM head of a trained
transformer, judged by replacement and selected-score reconstruction.
Activation SAEs learn overcomplete bases for the hidden-state factor
\(h\) of a lens score \(h^\top q\)
\citep{bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2023sparse,gao2024scaling,templeton2024scaling},
while SRP applies the same machinery to the complementary factor \(q\).
Parameter-space decompositions likewise factorize weights
\citep{braun2025parameter,gao2025weightsparse}, with SRP keeping the
model fixed and tying the factorization to the lens-score interface.

\paragraph{Additive attribution and mechanisms.}
Direct logit attribution splits a token logit or logit-difference
direction into contributions from residual-stream components
\citep{elhage2021framework,wang2022ioi}, extended across components by
logit prisms \citep{nguyen2024logitprisms}; composing the row-side
basis with DLA refines each component's scalar into signed per-feature
terms. Interventions \citep{conmy2023acdc}, causal abstraction
\citep{geiger2023causalabstraction}, and transcoder or
attribution-graph approaches
\citep{dunefsky2024transcoders,ameisen2025circuittracing} address
complementary mechanism-level questions.
Appendix~\ref{app:extended-related-work} gives broader positioning.
