\section{Evaluation Setup}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

We evaluate SRP in two stages. First, calibration asks whether the learned
readout basis is faithful enough to support local feature readings, by
testing LM-head preservation and reconstruction of selected readout scores.
Second, given these checks pass, the analyses ask what SRP adds beyond
logit-lens scores: how the basis responds to varied inputs (score choice,
token preference, prompt context), and how it supports attribution and
constrained readout-side edits. All claims are readout-level, except the
constrained-edit stress test, which additionally intervenes on the final
hidden state and reports distribution-shift diagnostics
(\Cref{subsec:constrained-readout-edits}).

\paragraph{Models and held-out sets.}
The suite covers Qwen3.5 \citep{qwen35collection}, Gemma-4
\citep{gemma4official,gemma4collection}, Ministral \citep{liu2026ministral3},
and reasoning-distilled Qwen/Llama readouts \citep{deepseekai2025deepseekr1};
identifiers and selection details are in
Appendix~\ref{app:model-suite-selection}. We fit one readout SAE per model to
the rows of its final LM head. Replacement fidelity uses 10,000 held-out C4 continuations per model
\citep{raffel2020exploring,dodge2021documenting}, and score reconstruction
uses fixed prompt/score sets.

\paragraph{Calibration metrics and baselines.}
Calibration covers basis quality, score quality, and baseline comparisons.
\emph{Basis} metrics test held-out row reconstruction and LM-head replacement
on C4 hidden states. \emph{Score} metrics test sign preservation and relative
error for selected logits, contrasts, and margins. \emph{Baselines} include
nearest-row ridge, matched-rank PCA, shuffled sparse codes, and random sparse
patterns. We interpret feature bars only for sign-preserved, low-error
accounts. Feature labels are descriptive aids; feature ids, signed
contributions, and residuals are the measured objects.
