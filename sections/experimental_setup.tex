\section{Evaluation Design}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

Each metric family tests one claim about the factorization:
\emph{faithfulness} (selected-score residual, sign preservation, and
replacement behavior), \emph{distinctness} (comparison against direct
row geometry and dense low-rank alternatives),
\emph{reproducibility} (independently trained dictionaries and held-out
group recovery), \emph{intervention relevance} (predicted versus
realized local readout-side changes), and \emph{utility} (feature-level
analyses that token rankings cannot express,
\Cref{sec:beyond-token}).

\paragraph{Models, gate, and held-out sets.}
The suite spans Qwen3.5~\citep{qwen35collection}, Gemma-4
\citep{gemma4official,gemma4collection}, Ministral~\citep{liu2026ministral3},
and reasoning-distilled Qwen/Llama readouts \citep{deepseekai2025deepseekr1};
identifiers and selection criteria appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:model-suite-selection}. Because decomposing a fixed readout
needs only the final weights, the suite is chosen by recency and spans three
model families with base, instruction-tuned, and reasoning-distilled variants.
We fit one readout factorizer per model to the rows of its final LM head and
evaluate it on 10{,}000 held-out C4 continuations per model
\citep{raffel2020exploring,dodge2021documenting} together with a fixed
prompt-and-score set. The model split is fixed once: all eight models enter
the reconstruction analysis, and the six whose readouts pass the
interpretation gate (held-out replacement top-1 agreement \(\ge 0.75\);
both Gemma-4 variants fall below it) enter the baseline, reproducibility,
and intervention comparisons.

\paragraph{Contrast banks.}
Selected scores come from fixed, model-agnostic banks
(Appendix~\ref{app:query-bank-provenance}). A hand-written set of 301
prompts spanning factual QA, instruction, paragraph, code, dialogue,
retrieval-update, and safety/tool settings supplies model-native contrasts
built from each model's own ranking: top-1 against the vocabulary mean, a
row-norm-matched distractor, a sampled competitor, and the rank-5 and
rank-2 tokens. A curated A/B bank of 320 template-expanded records,
supplemented by 40 case-candidate records of the same types,
supplies the current-vs-stale, source-vs-prior, action/tool, and
abstention families; per-tokenizer single-token filters on
length, frequency, whitespace, capitalization, row norm, special-token
status, and collisions
leave 82--96 cases per family. The banks are held out by construction:
factorizers are trained on LM-head rows and selected on
row-reconstruction, replacement, and code-usage metrics, with the
contrast set reserved for post-selection evaluation.
Aggregates cluster items by base case (one prompt--score template with
its variants), the resampling unit for bootstrap intervals; the baseline
comparisons use the full \(\sim\)1{,}350-item set per model over
\(\sim\)850 base cases. A separate benchmark-derived bank contributes 300
fixed row combinations for each of two Qwen models
(\Cref{subsec:selected-score-reconstruction}).
