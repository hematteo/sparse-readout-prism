\section{Evaluation Setup}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

We evaluate SRP in two stages. \emph{Calibration} tests whether the learned
readout basis is faithful enough to support local feature readings,
measuring LM-head preservation and reconstruction of selected readout
scores. \emph{Analyses}, run only after calibration passes, ask what SRP
adds beyond logit-lens scores: how the basis responds to varied inputs
(score choice, token preference, prompt context), and how it supports
attribution and constrained readout-side edits. All claims are
readout-level. The one exception is the constrained-edit experiment
(\Cref{subsec:constrained-readout-edits}), which intervenes on the final
hidden state and reports distribution-shift diagnostics.

\paragraph{Models and held-out sets.}
The suite spans Qwen3.5 \citep{qwen35collection}, Gemma-4
\citep{gemma4official,gemma4collection}, Ministral \citep{liu2026ministral3},
and reasoning-distilled Qwen/Llama readouts \citep{deepseekai2025deepseekr1};
identifiers and selection criteria appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:model-suite-selection}. We fit one readout SAE per model
to the rows of its final LM head. Replacement fidelity is measured on
10,000 held-out C4 continuations per model
\citep{raffel2020exploring,dodge2021documenting}; score reconstruction is
evaluated on a fixed set of prompts and scores.

\paragraph{Calibration metrics and baselines.}
Calibration has three components. \emph{Basis} metrics measure held-out
row reconstruction and LM-head replacement on C4 hidden states.
\emph{Score} metrics measure sign preservation and relative error for
selected logits, contrasts, and margins. \emph{Baselines} are
nearest-row ridge, matched-rank PCA, shuffled sparse codes, and random
sparse patterns. We interpret feature contributions only for
sign-preserved, low-error accounts; feature labels serve as descriptive
aids, while the measured objects are feature ids, signed contributions,
and residuals.
