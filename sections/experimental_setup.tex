\section{Evaluation Setup}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

We evaluate SRP in two stages. First, calibration tests whether the learned
readout basis preserves the LM head and reconstructs selected readout scores
well enough to support local feature readings. Second, conditional on these
checks, the analyses ask what SRP adds beyond logit-lens scores: how score
choice, token preference, prompt context, residual-stream attribution, and
constrained readout-side edits appear in the readout-feature basis. All
analyses are readout-level except the constrained-edit stress test in
\Cref{subsec:constrained-readout-edits}.

\paragraph{Models and held-out sets.}
The suite covers Qwen3.5 \citep{qwen35collection}, Gemma-4
\citep{gemma4official,gemma4collection}, Ministral \citep{liu2026ministral3},
and reasoning-distilled Qwen/Llama readouts \citep{deepseekai2025deepseekr1};
identifiers and selection details are in
Appendix~\ref{app:model-suite-selection}. We fit readout SAEs on final LM-head
inputs. Replacement fidelity uses 10,000 held-out C4 continuations per model
\citep{raffel2020exploring,dodge2021documenting}; score reconstruction uses
fixed prompt/score sets with prespecified coefficients, split assignments, and
tokenization filters.

\paragraph{Calibration metrics and baselines.}
Calibration has three gates. \emph{Basis} metrics test held-out row
reconstruction and LM-head replacement on C4 hidden states. \emph{Score}
metrics test sign preservation and relative error for selected logits,
contrasts, and margins. \emph{Baselines} compare SRP with nearest-row ridge,
matched-rank PCA, shuffled sparse codes, and random sparse patterns. We read
feature bars only for sign-preserved, low-error accounts; feature labels are
descriptive aids, while feature ids, signed contributions, and residuals are
the measured objects.
