\section{Evaluation Setup}
\label{sec:experimental-setup}

We evaluate SRP in two stages. \emph{Calibration} measures row, replacement,
and selected-score fidelity for the learned readout basis. \emph{Analyses} use
the calibrated operating regime to ask what SRP adds beyond logit-lens scores:
how the basis responds to varied inputs (score choice, token preference, prompt
context), and how it supports attribution and constrained readout-side edits.
The constrained-edit experiment (\Cref{subsec:constrained-readout-edits}) also
intervenes on the final hidden state and reports distribution-shift diagnostics.

\paragraph{Models and held-out sets.}
The suite spans Qwen3.5~\citep{qwen35collection}, Gemma-4
\citep{gemma4official,gemma4collection}, Ministral~\citep{liu2026ministral3},
and reasoning-distilled Qwen/Llama readouts \citep{deepseekai2025deepseekr1};
identifiers and selection criteria appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:model-suite-selection}. Because decomposing a fixed readout
needs only the final weights, the suite is chosen by recency and spans three
model families with base, instruction-tuned, and reasoning-distilled variants. We fit one readout factorizer per
model to the rows of its final LM head and evaluate it on 10{,}000
held-out C4 continuations per model
\citep{raffel2020exploring,dodge2021documenting} together with a fixed
prompt-and-score set.

\paragraph{Contrast banks.}
Selected scores come from fixed, model-agnostic banks
(Appendix~\ref{app:query-bank-provenance}). A hand-written set of 301
prompts spanning factual QA, instruction, paragraph, code, dialogue,
retrieval-update, and safety/tool settings supplies model-native contrasts
built from each model's own ranking: top-1 against the vocabulary mean, a
row-norm-matched distractor, a sampled competitor, and the rank-5 and
rank-2 tokens. A curated A/B bank of 320 template-expanded records,
supplemented by 40 case-candidate records of the same types,
supplies the current-vs-stale, source-vs-prior, action/tool, and
abstention families; per-tokenizer single-token filters on
length, frequency, whitespace, capitalization, row norm, special-token
status, and collisions
leave 82--96 cases per family. The banks are held out by construction:
factorizers are trained on LM-head rows and selected on
row-reconstruction, replacement, and code-usage metrics, with the
contrast set reserved for post-selection evaluation.
Aggregates cluster items by base case (one prompt--score template with
its variants), the resampling unit for bootstrap intervals; the Qwen3.5-2B
baseline comparison uses the full 1{,}348-item set over 848 base cases. A
separate benchmark-derived bank contributes 300 fixed row combinations
for each of two Qwen models
(\Cref{subsec:selected-score-reconstruction}).

\paragraph{Calibration metrics and baselines.}
Calibration has three components. \emph{Basis} metrics measure held-out
row reconstruction and LM-head replacement. \emph{Score} metrics measure
sign preservation and relative error for selected logits, contrasts, and
margins. \emph{Baselines} are nearest-row ridge, dense PCA references
(including a point matched to SRP's realized per-score compactness),
shuffled sparse codes, and random sparse patterns. Feature contributions are
reported for sign-preserved, low-error accounts; feature labels serve as
descriptive aids, while the measured objects are feature ids, signed
contributions, and residuals.
